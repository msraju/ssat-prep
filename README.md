1. **Exact 50 Questions & 30-Minute Timing**: Covers 100% of all vocabulary words.

   - **SSAT Elementary**: 25 Synonyms + 25 Analogies (5 choices A, B, C, D, E)
   - **ISEE Middle Level**: 25 Synonyms + 25 Sentence Completions (4 choices A, B, C, D)

   - Flip-cards with definitions, parts of speech, and key synonyms for all 100 words.
