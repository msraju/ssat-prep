# SSAT Elementary & ISEE Middle Level Verbal Prep Portal

An interactive, timed verbal reasoning practice portal covering **100 essential vocabulary words** across both major independent school entrance exams:

- 🔵 **SSAT Elementary Level (50 Words)**: Synonyms & Analogies (5 choices A–E)
- 🟣 **ISEE Middle Level (50 Words)**: Synonyms & Sentence Completion (4 choices A–D)

---

## 🌐 Live Interactive Test
Take the timed test on any browser (iPad, tablet, phone, or laptop):  
👉 **[https://msraju.github.io/ssat-prep/](https://msraju.github.io/ssat-prep/)**

---

## 📚 Features

1. **Dual Exam Switcher**: Toggle between SSAT Elementary and ISEE Middle Level instantly.
2. **100% Word Coverage**: Every single vocabulary word from both study sheets is actively tested.
3. **Authentic Official Formats**:
   - **SSAT Elementary**: 15 Synonyms + 15 Analogies (20-min timed simulation or 50-word master exam)
   - **ISEE Middle Level**: 20 Synonyms + 20 Sentence Completions (20-min timed simulation or 50-word master exam)
4. **Automated Grading & Scoring**:
   - Scaled score estimates and percentile / stanine rankings
   - Detailed bridge explanations for analogies and context clues for sentence completions
5. **Interactive Flashcards**:
   - Flip-cards with definitions, parts of speech, and key synonyms for all 100 words.
6. **Printable Test Papers**:
   - [SSAT Elementary Practice Test (Markdown)](./SSAT_Elementary_Verbal_Practice_Test.md)
   - [ISEE Middle Level Practice Test (Markdown)](./ISEE_Middle_Level_Verbal_Practice_Test.md)
