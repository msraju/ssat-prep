# SECTION 2: SENTENCE COMPLETION (Questions 26–50)
*Directions: Each sentence below has one or two blanks, each blank indicating that something has been omitted. Choose the word or set of words that best fits the meaning of the sentence as a whole.*

(A) repel  
(B) lure  
(C) frighten  
(D) warn  

**27.** Because the travelers had only a ________ supply of fresh water left in the desert, they were forced to ration every sip.  
(A) meager  
(B) plentiful  
(C) luxurious  
(D) vibrant  

**28.** The clever parrot was able to ________ the sound of the ringing telephone with astonishing accuracy.  
(A) omit  
(B) mimic  
(C) destroy  
(D) avoid

(A) noncommittal  
(B) decisive  
(C) furious  
(D) braggart

(A) unknown  
(B) notorious

(D) fragile

(A) jovial
